# FRAME / FORM — Direction 02

## Visual direction

An independent creative portfolio with editorial typography, generous image areas and a restrained cobalt identity. Off-white paper and dark ink keep the owner's work prominent. The oversized name, blue film frame and asymmetric collection grid establish the visual signature.

The homepage follows a recruiter-friendly sequence: identity, selected work, personal introduction, capabilities, experience and contact. Collection pages separate role, tools, process and finished work. AI-assisted films are explicitly presented as a separate discipline.

## Design system

- Paper: `#f4f4ef`; ink: `#171916`; cobalt: `#244cf0`.
- Display: Barlow Condensed, locally bundled. Interface: Archivo Variable, locally bundled. Traditional Chinese uses available Traditional Chinese system fonts.
- English and Traditional Chinese have separate heading-size rules. Translation must cover controls, alt text, status messages and metadata, not just headings.
- Motion is limited to short entry transitions, hover feedback and the owner's video excerpt. No custom cursor, scroll hijacking, particle overlays or decorative generated imagery.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable automatic video playback and animated transitions. Explicit playback remains available.

## Asset and content rules

Use registered stable asset IDs. Keep full-resolution sources untouched and use responsive derivatives in layouts. Video galleries do not preload full films. The homepage uses a short silent derivative, not the full source file.

Keep descriptions grounded in the work. Do not add invented clients, awards, performance metrics or employment claims. New external assets require a documented source and licence; supplementary visuals must never be represented as the owner's commissioned work.

## Interaction requirements

Preserve keyboard focus indicators, the skip link, labelled language controls, mobile navigation, native capability disclosures, fullscreen image navigation, Escape-to-close, original-file access and video playback controls. Keep contact and résumé accessible from collection pages as well as the homepage.
